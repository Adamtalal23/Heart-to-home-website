About us

A family business, built one home at a time.

Family owned, locally rooted, and still on every job.

Heart To Home is a family-owned business. Adam Talal started it from the ground up, and our family lives right here in Maricopa County.

Our motto, "From our heart to your home," says what we believe: the work should come from the heart, and it should leave your house feeling more like home. We keep our crew small on purpose. The person who quotes your job is the person doing the work, so nothing gets lost between a salesperson, a manager and a crew you've never met.

We also paint new homes for a regional home builder, so we work to builder schedules and builder standards every week. At your house, we bring that same speed with the care of a custom finish.

And we proudly use Sherwin-Williams paint on our projects.

Questions

Frequently asked

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We are licensed with the Arizona Registrar of Contractors (AZ ROC #342540), bonded and insured. You can look up our license on the ROC website.

Who gets the 10% hero discount?

Military (active duty and veterans), first responders and educators get 10% off their project. Just mention it when you request your estimate.

What paint do you use?

We proudly use Sherwin-Williams paint. Pick any Sherwin-Williams color and we'll bring it to life.

Why paint before moving in or out?

An empty house is the easiest house to paint. There's no furniture to move or cover, every wall is easy to reach, and the job is done before you settle in or hand over the keys.

Are estimates really free?

Yes. We come out, look at the space, and send a written, fixed-price quote with no obligation.

Do you handle HOA color approvals?

For exterior work we can help you pull your HOA's approved color schemes and prepare the submission.

Do I need to move furniture?

No. We move and cover furniture as part of the job. We just ask that small items, decor and valuables be put away.

Do you use subcontractors?

Rarely. Painting, flooring, trim, kitchen installs, bathroom work and handyman jobs are done by our own crew. If a project needs a licensed plumber or electrician, we tell you up front and coordinate it for you.

Can you handle a list of small jobs?

Yes. Send us your honey-do list and we'll quote the whole list and knock it out in one visit.
